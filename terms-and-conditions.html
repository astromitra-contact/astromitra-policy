4 Accuracy of your chart

  • Birth charts depend directly on the precision of the details you enter. Inaccurate birth data will yield an inaccurate astrological chart.
  • If you do not know your exact time of birth, the application assumes noon (12:00 PM) as a default. In this scenario, your Lagna (Ascendant) and house placements may be imprecise, and the chart will be marked as approximate.
  • Birth places are resolved to coordinates and time zones via a third-party geographic lookup service (GeoNames). When entering your birth location, please provide clear city, state, and country names to avoid ambiguity.
  • AstroMitra uses the widely accepted Lahiri Ayanamsa with the Whole-Sign / Vedic Bhavas calculation method. Variations in ayanamsas or house systems used by other astrologers or software may produce differing chart interpretations.
